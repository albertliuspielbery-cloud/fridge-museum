English

拍下孩子的畫作和勞作，自動拉正、去黃、變白，勞作自動去背，依孩子、年齡和學期整理收藏。

拍照小技巧

  • 畫作：整張紙入鏡，四周留一點桌面；盡量避免手機的影子蓋到紙上。
  • 勞作：放在素色桌面上，四周留空，不要碰到畫面邊緣。
  • 四角沒對準時，可以在「裁切」裡拖曳四個圓點；方向不對時，可以旋轉。

常見問題

作品存到哪裡？
存在 App 裡；按「存到手機」會存進「照片」App。允許完整照片權限時，會放進每個孩子專屬的相簿。

照片會上傳嗎？
不會。所有處理都在您的 iPhone 上完成。詳見隱私權政策。

淡色的蠟筆被洗掉了？
在「調整」裡把「保護淡色」調高，或把「紙張變白」調低。

My Fridge Museum · Support

© Starlight Studio

Photograph your child’s drawings and crafts. Drawings are straightened and their yellowed paper turned white; crafts are cut out from the table. Everything is organized by child, age, and semester.

Photo tips

  • Drawings: get the whole sheet in the frame, with a little table showing around it. Try not to let the phone’s shadow fall on the paper.
  • Crafts: use a plain table and leave space around the piece, away from the edges of the frame.
  • If the corners are off, drag the four dots in “Crop.” If it’s the wrong way round, rotate it.

FAQ

Where are my artworks saved?
In the app. “Save to Photos” adds them to the Photos app; with full Photos access, each child gets their own album.

Are my photos uploaded?
No. Everything is processed on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

Pale crayon got washed out?
In “Adjust,” raise “Protect Pale Colors” or lower “Whiten Paper.”
