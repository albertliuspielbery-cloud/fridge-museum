冰箱美術館・隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 29 日　© 星光工作室 Starlight Studio

English

「冰箱美術館」讓家長用 iPhone 拍下孩子的畫作和勞作，整理、保存成數位作品集。孩子的作品照片非常私密，所以這個 App 的設計原則是：所有處理都在您的 iPhone 上完成，我們（開發者）不會收到您的任何照片或資料。如果您開啟「iCloud 同步」（完整版功能，可選），作品會存到您自己的 iCloud，只有您登入同一個 Apple 帳號的裝置看得到，我們看不到。

我們不收集任何個人資料

  • 不需要註冊帳號，不會要求電子郵件、電話或任何聯絡方式。
  • 沒有廣告、沒有追蹤工具、沒有分析工具，不會把任何資料傳給我們或第三方。
  • App 不連網就能使用拍照、整理、存到手機等功能；只有購買完整版（透過 App Store）和 iCloud 同步需要連網。

只存在您自己裝置（和您自己的 iCloud）上的資料

下面這些資料存在這台 iPhone 上；開啟 iCloud 同步時，也會存到您自己的 iCloud：

  • 作品的原始照片和處理後的照片，以及孩子的頭像
  • 您輸入的孩子名字（或暱稱）、生日、作品名稱、標籤、備註
  • 作品的語音小故事錄音和轉出的文字

沒有開啟 iCloud 同步時，刪除 App 會一起刪除這些資料。如果您的 iPhone 有開啟 iCloud 備份，這些資料可能會包含在 Apple 為您保管的裝置備份裡，這部分由 Apple 依其隱私權政策處理，我們無法存取。

iCloud 同步（完整版，可選）

  • 開啟後，作品（原始照片、處理後的照片、縮圖、語音小故事）和孩子的名字、生日、頭像、作品名稱、標籤、備註，會透過 Apple 的 CloudKit 存到您自己 iCloud 帳號的私人資料庫，讓您用同一個 Apple 帳號的 iPhone、iPad 都看得到。這些資料由 Apple 依其隱私權政策保管，佔用您的 iCloud 空間；我們（開發者）無法讀取，也不會收到。
  • 不會和其他 Apple 帳號分享（家人共享只共享完整版的購買，不共享作品）。
  • 隨時可以在 App 的「設定」關閉，下次打開 App 時生效；關閉同步不會刪掉手機上的作品。
  • 請注意：如果在 App 開著、同步也開著時登出 iCloud（或在 iPhone「設定」關掉這個 App 的 iCloud），iOS 會依 Apple 的設計把已同步的資料從這支手機移除；已上傳的資料仍在您的 iCloud，重新登入同一個帳號就會回來；還沒上傳完成的作品或修改（例如剛拍、沒網路時）可能會遺失。想多一份保險，可以用「存到手機」把作品存進「照片」。

相機

只用來拍攝孩子的作品。照片在手機上處理，不會傳給我們；開啟 iCloud 同步時，作品會存到您自己的 iCloud。

照片

  • 從相簿匯入作品時，只讀取您選擇的照片。
  • 「存到手機」會把作品存進「照片」App。如果您允許完整照片權限，App 會幫每個孩子建立一本「冰箱美術館 – 名字」相簿；不允許的話，作品會直接存進「最近項目」。

麥克風和語音辨識

只有在您錄「語音小故事」時才會使用麥克風。錄音存在這台裝置上（開啟 iCloud 同步時也會存到您自己的 iCloud）；轉成文字使用 iPhone 內建的裝置端語音辨識，不會傳出去。您可以隨時在「設定」裡關閉麥克風權限，其他功能不受影響。

分享

只有在您按下「分享」時，App 才會打開 iPhone 內建的分享選單，由您決定要傳給誰。App 本身不會把作品傳到任何地方。

兒童隱私

這個 App 是給家長使用的。孩子不需要、也不能建立帳號。

My Fridge Museum · Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026　© Starlight Studio

My Fridge Museum lets parents photograph their children’s drawings and crafts and keep them as a digital collection. Children’s artwork is personal, so the app is built on one rule: everything is processed on your iPhone, and we (the developer) never receive your photos or data. If you turn on iCloud Sync (an optional Full Version feature), artworks are kept in your own iCloud, visible only on devices signed in to your Apple Account. We can’t see them.

We don’t collect any personal data

  • No account is needed. The app never asks for an email address, phone number, or any contact details.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. No data is sent to us or to any third party.
  • Taking, editing, and saving artworks work without an internet connection. Only buying the Full Version (through the App Store) and iCloud Sync need one.

Data that stays on your device (and in your own iCloud)

The following is stored on this iPhone, and also in your own iCloud when iCloud Sync is on:

  • The original and processed photos of each artwork, and each child’s avatar
  • The children’s names (or nicknames) and birthdays, and the titles, tags, and notes you enter
  • Voice notes and the text transcribed from them

Without iCloud Sync, deleting the app deletes this data. If iCloud Backup is on, it may be included in the device backup Apple keeps for you; that backup is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy, and we can’t access it.

iCloud Sync (Full Version, optional)

  • When it’s on, artworks (original and processed photos, thumbnails, voice notes) and the children’s names, birthdays, avatars, titles, tags, and notes are stored through Apple’s CloudKit in the private database of your own iCloud account, so you can see them on any iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple keeps this data under its own privacy policy, and it uses your iCloud storage. We (the developer) can’t read it and never receive it.
  • Nothing is shared with other Apple Accounts (Family Sharing shares the Full Version purchase, not artworks).
  • You can turn it off in the app’s Settings at any time; it takes effect the next time you open the app. Turning sync off never deletes artworks on the phone.
  • Please note: if you sign out of iCloud (or turn off iCloud for this app in the iPhone’s Settings) while the app is open with sync on, iOS removes the synced data from that phone, as Apple designed it. What was already uploaded is still in your iCloud and comes back when you sign in with the same account; artworks or changes not uploaded yet (for example, just taken or made offline) may be lost. For an extra copy, use “Save to Photos.”

Camera

Used only to photograph your child’s artwork. Photos are processed on the phone and never sent to us; with iCloud Sync on, artworks are kept in your own iCloud.

Photos

When you import from your library, the app reads only the photos you pick. “Save to Photos” adds artworks to the Photos app, only when you tap it. With full Photos access, the app creates an album for each child (“My Fridge Museum – name”); with add-only access, artworks go to Recents. The app never reads or uploads the rest of your library.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only while you record a voice note. Recordings stay on this device (and in your own iCloud when iCloud Sync is on). Transcription uses the iPhone’s built-in on-device speech recognition and is never sent anywhere; on models that can’t transcribe offline, only the recording is kept. You can turn off microphone access in Settings at any time; everything else keeps working.

Sharing

Only when you tap “Share” does the app open the iPhone share sheet, and you choose where it goes. The app itself never sends artwork anywhere.

Children’s privacy

This app is for parents. Children don’t need, and can’t create, an account.
