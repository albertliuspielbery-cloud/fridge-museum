冰箱美術館・隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 29 日　© 星光工作室 Starlight Studio

English

「冰箱美術館」讓家長用 iPhone 拍下孩子的畫作和勞作，整理、保存成數位作品集。孩子的作品照片非常私密，所以這個 App 的設計原則是：所有處理都在您的 iPhone 上完成，不上傳到任何地方。

我們不收集任何個人資料

  • 不需要註冊帳號，不會要求電子郵件、電話或任何聯絡方式。
  • 沒有廣告、沒有追蹤工具、沒有分析工具，不會把任何資料傳給我們或第三方。
  • App 不連網就能使用全部功能（購買完整版需透過 App Store）。

只存在您自己裝置上的資料

下面這些資料只存在這台 iPhone 上：

  • 作品的原始照片和處理後的照片，以及孩子的頭像
  • 您輸入的孩子名字（或暱稱）、生日、作品名稱、標籤、備註
  • 作品的語音小故事錄音和轉出的文字

刪除 App 時，這些資料會一起刪除。如果您的 iPhone 有開啟 iCloud 備份，這些資料可能會包含在 Apple 為您保管的裝置備份裡，這部分由 Apple 依其隱私權政策處理，我們無法存取。

相機

只用來拍攝孩子的作品。照片在手機上處理，不會傳出去。

照片

  • 從相簿匯入作品時，只讀取您選擇的照片。
  • 「存到手機」會把作品存進「照片」App。如果您允許完整照片權限，App 會幫每個孩子建立一本「冰箱美術館 – 名字」相簿；不允許的話，作品會直接存進「最近項目」。

麥克風和語音辨識

只有在您錄「語音小故事」時才會使用麥克風。錄音只存在這台裝置上；轉成文字使用 iPhone 內建的裝置端語音辨識，不會傳出去。您可以隨時在「設定」裡關閉麥克風權限，其他功能不受影響。

分享

只有在您按下「分享」時，App 才會打開 iPhone 內建的分享選單，由您決定要傳給誰。App 本身不會把作品傳到任何地方。

兒童隱私

這個 App 是給家長使用的。孩子不需要、也不能建立帳號。

My Fridge Museum · Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026　© Starlight Studio

My Fridge Museum lets parents photograph their children’s drawings and crafts and keep them as a digital collection. Children’s artwork is personal, so the app is built on one rule: everything is processed on your iPhone, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

We don’t collect any personal data

  • No account is needed. The app never asks for an email address, phone number, or any contact details.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. No data is sent to us or to any third party.
  • Every feature works without an internet connection (buying the Full Version goes through the App Store).

Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only on this iPhone:

  • The original and processed photos of each artwork, and each child’s avatar
  • The children’s names (or nicknames) and birthdays, and the titles, tags, and notes you enter
  • Voice notes and the text transcribed from them

Deleting the app deletes this data. If iCloud Backup is on, it may be included in the device backup Apple keeps for you; that backup is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy, and we can’t access it.

Camera

Used only to photograph your child’s artwork. Photos are processed on the phone and never sent anywhere.

Photos

When you import from your library, the app reads only the photos you pick. “Save to Photos” adds artworks to the Photos app, only when you tap it. With full Photos access, the app creates an album for each child (“My Fridge Museum – name”); with add-only access, artworks go to Recents. The app never reads or uploads the rest of your library.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only while you record a voice note. Recordings stay on this device. Transcription uses the iPhone’s built-in on-device speech recognition and is never sent anywhere; on models that can’t transcribe offline, only the recording is kept. You can turn off microphone access in Settings at any time; everything else keeps working.

Sharing

Only when you tap “Share” does the app open the iPhone share sheet, and you choose where it goes. The app itself never sends artwork anywhere.

Children’s privacy

This app is for parents. Children don’t need, and can’t create, an account.
